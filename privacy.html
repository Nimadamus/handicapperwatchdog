Privacy Policy

Draft for your review. Not in effect until you approve it.

Last updated October 26, 2026.

What we collect

If you browse without an account, the site hosts keep standard connection logs. Those logs include IP address, browser type, and the page requested. Google Analytics records page visits. We do not require an account to view the free rankings. If you subscribe, we keep your email address, your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, your plan and its status, and the dates it renews or ends. We do not receive or store your card number. Stripe processes payments.

How we use it

To run your account and Pro access, send sign in links, purchase confirmations, and messages about renewal, failed payment, or the end of a subscription, prevent abuse, and understand which pages are used. We also email ourselves when a new subscription starts. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that run the service for us: Stripe (payments and billing), Cloudflare (hosting the account service), GitHub (hosting the site), Google Analytics (visit statistics) and Brevo (sign in and account emails).

Cookies

Signing in sets one first party cookie named hwd_s. It is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite Lax, and it lasts 30 days. It holds a signed session identifier, not a password. Google Analytics sets its own cookies to count visits. Checkout and the billing portal run on Stripe, which sets its own cookies. We do not use advertising cookies.

Records about handicappers

Handicapper records on the site come from picks posted publicly on third party platforms.

Retention

We keep account data while your account exists and as long as needed for tax, accounting and legal reasons after it closes.

Your choices and rights

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account page. To see, correct or delete your account data, email support@handicapperwatchdog.com. California residents may request access to and deletion of their personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

Children

The service is for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
