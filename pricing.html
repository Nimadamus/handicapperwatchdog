Questions

Where do the records come from?

We capture picks that handicappers post in public on X, Covers, Reddit, TheRX, Sportsbook Review and Predictem, and grade each one against the final score. We track thousands of handicappers, not every one on the internet.

What counts as a pick?

Straight bets posted before the game started. Held, duplicate and unreviewed posts are left out. Units assume one unit risked per pick at the posted price.

How do I cancel?

From your account page, open billing and cancel. Pro stays on until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

Is this betting advice?

No. Records are past results, not predictions or guarantees. Bet only what you can afford to lose. 21+.
