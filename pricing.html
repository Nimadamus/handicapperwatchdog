Questions

Where do the records come from?

We capture picks handicappers post in public on X, Covers, Reddit, TheRX, Sportsbook Review and Predictem, and grade each one against the final score. We track thousands of handicappers, not every one on the internet.

What counts as a graded pick?

Straight bets posted before the game started. Held, duplicate and unreviewed posts are left out. Units assume one unit risked per pick at the posted price.

How do I cancel?

From your account page, open billing and cancel. Pro stays on until the end of the period you already paid for and you are not charged again.

Can I switch from monthly to annual?

Yes. Open billing from your account page and change your plan there.

Do past results predict future results?

No. Records are past results, not predictions or guarantees. Pro gives you the full history so you can judge for yourself. Bet only what you can afford to lose. 21+.

Is my payment secure?

Checkout and billing run on Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers.
