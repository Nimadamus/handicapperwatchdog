Methodology

01Captured from public posts

Picks are harvested daily from the public places handicappers post them, with the time each was posted. Picks posted after the game starts are not counted.

02Graded on the final score

Straight bets are graded against final scores. Units assume 1 unit risked per pick at the posted price, or a reference price when none was posted.

03Sample size on every record

Small sample under 20 picks, Developing 20 to 99, Established 100 or more. Pending picks are never mixed into records.

Handicapper Watchdog grades picks captured from public posts on X, Covers, Reddit, TheRX, Sportsbook Review and Predictem. Records are past results, not predictions or guarantees. Held, duplicate and unreviewed picks are not counted.
