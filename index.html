Graded picks on the record go back to September 2026. Investigate the tracked record before you trust an advertised one.

    Before you rely on a record.

    Can I find out who a handicapper is?

    No. Names, account names and account links are not published on the free site or in Watchdog Pro. Each account is a permanent Watchdog ID instead.

    Does Watchdog track every handicapper?

    No. It tracks public accounts it has found on X, Reddit, Covers, TheRX, Sportsbook Review and Predictem. Private groups, paid channels and other sites are not covered.

    Does a good record mean future winners?

    No. A record is history. Results swing, especially on small samples, and a handicapper who is up today can lose tomorrow. Nothing on this site is a promise of profit.

    Can a handicapper pay to rank higher?

    No. Rank comes only from graded results. There are no paid placements, featured rows or verified badges for sale.

    What does a pending pick include in Pro?

    The sport, game, pick and odds as posted, the post time, and the Watchdog ID with its record. Not the name or the original post.

    How often does the site update?

    The board and profiles rebuild from the tracker about every 30 minutes. The time of the last capture is shown with the statistics on this page.
