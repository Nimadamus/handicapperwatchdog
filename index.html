How it works

From a public post to a graded record

Watchdog does the bookkeeping a handicapper’s own promotion leaves out. Six steps, the same for every account.

  1. 1Discover

    We find accounts that post picks in public on X, Reddit, Covers, TheRX, Sportsbook Review and Predictem. Thousands of accounts have been found so far.

  2. 2Monitor

    Accounts that post picks are watched. Their posts are read on a regular schedule, so the losing picks are captured along with the winners.

  3. 3Capture before the start

    A pick is saved with the time it was posted. If that time is after the game started, it does not count.

  4. 4Grade independently

    When the game is final, the pick is graded against the final score: win, loss or push. Held, duplicate and unreviewed posts are left out.

  5. 5Calculate the record

    Record, units, ROI, win rate, streaks and form by sport, rebuilt from the graded picks every time the site updates.

  6. 6Open the research

    Rankings and profiles are free. Watchdog Pro adds eligible pending picks before the start and the full pick log.

Why it matters

A winning record is easy to advertise

Many records you see online are kept by the person selling the picks. That is the problem this site exists to fix.

Cherry picked wins

Wins get reposted and pinned. Losses scroll away.

Every eligible pick we capture from a tracked account is graded, not just the ones shared again.
Deleted losers

A losing post can be deleted after the game.

We save the pick when we capture it. Deleting the post later does not remove it from the record.
Selective recordkeeping

A “last 30 days” window starts right after a cold streak.

Lifetime, 30 day and 7 day records sit side by side, with the sample size on each.
Picks after the fact

Some “records” include picks revealed once the game is underway.

A pick only counts if its post time is before the start.
Unverified win rates

“68% winners” says nothing about price or how many picks.

Units and ROI account for the odds. The number of graded picks is always shown.

What we do not cover. Watchdog tracks the public accounts it has found on the six sources above. It does not see private groups, paid channels, text messages or sites outside that list, and it cannot read every post on the sites it does cover. A record here covers the picks we captured, not every pick a person has ever made.

Why are handicappers anonymous?

The record is public. The identity is not, anywhere.

Handicapper Watchdog tracks public predictions and evaluates their results independently. Each tracked account receives a persistent anonymous Watchdog ID that never changes, so you can follow the same record for months.

We protect the commercial value of our research by not revealing the identities behind tracked profiles. If names were published, anyone could buy one month, copy the entire list of accounts worth following, cancel, and keep using it for free.

Subscribers get the premium research and tracking, including eligible pending picks, tied to the same Watchdog IDs. Subscribing does not unlock names or links to accounts. No plan includes a directory of real world identities.

Real records

What a graded record looks like

Two records from today’s board, chosen by rule: the established record with the most graded picks, and the established record with the most units lost. Both are real and both keep changing.

Units

Profit counted as if 1 unit were risked on every pick at the posted odds. +5 units means 5 times the standard stake was won overall.

ROI

Units won divided by units risked. +5% means 5 cents of profit for every dollar risked. It lets small and large samples be compared fairly.

Win rate

Wins divided by wins plus losses. At typical odds of minus 110 a bettor needs about 52.4% just to break even, so a high win rate alone proves little.

Streak

The current run of straight wins (W) or losses (L). Streaks are fun to watch and mean very little on their own.

Sample size

Small sample under 20 graded picks, Developing 20 to 99, Established 100 or more. Small samples swing on luck.

Our data

Why these records are different

Graded by us

Results come from final scores, not from the handicapper’s recap. Units use the posted odds, or a reference price when no odds were posted.

Timestamp checked

Every pick carries its post time. Picks posted after the start are excluded before grading.

History kept

Records build from the first capture forward. Old picks are not quietly dropped when a cold streak arrives.

Tracked by sport

An NFL bettor who also loses on hockey shows both. Sport records are split on every profile.

Analysis, not hype

Sample size labels, ROI, drawdowns and form windows sit beside the headline number.

Grading is automated and reviewed, and mistakes are possible. If you see one, send a correction. Coverage is limited to the sources listed above.

Questions

Before you rely on a record

Can I find out who a handicapper is?

No. Names, account names and account links are not published on the free site or in Watchdog Pro. Each account is a permanent Watchdog ID instead.

Does Watchdog track every handicapper?

No. It tracks public accounts it has found on X, Reddit, Covers, TheRX, Sportsbook Review and Predictem. Private groups, paid channels and other sites are not covered.

Does a good record mean future winners?

No. A record is history. Results swing, especially on small samples, and a handicapper who is up today can lose tomorrow. Nothing on this site is a promise of profit.

Can a handicapper pay to rank higher?

No. Rank comes only from graded results. There are no paid placements, featured rows or verified badges for sale.

What does a pending pick include in Pro?

The sport, game, pick and odds as posted, the post time, and the Watchdog ID with its record. Not the name or the original post.

How often does the site update?

The board and profiles rebuild from the tracker about every 30 minutes. The time of the last capture is shown at the top of this page.
