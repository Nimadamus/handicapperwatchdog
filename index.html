    Before you buy picks

    Five Questions to Ask Before Paying Any Handicapper

    Any honest seller should be able to answer all five. Bring them to every service you consider.

    Should you pay for picks?
    1. 1

      Who kept this record?

      If the seller keeps the books, you are reading marketing. A record only means something when someone else wrote it down at the time.

      How Watchdog helps

      Watchdog captures public picks before the start and grades them from final scores.

      How we grade
    2. 2

      Can you see all of it?

      A record that starts last month, or leaves out a sport, is a highlight reel. Ask for the full history, not a good stretch.

      How Watchdog helps

      Lifetime, 90, 30 and 7 day windows sit side by side on every profile.

      Open the rankings
    3. 3

      Is it profitable after the odds?

      Win rate is not profit. At minus 110 a bettor needs about 52.4% just to break even, so ask for units and ROI.

      How Watchdog helps

      Every record shows units won or lost and return on investment.

      Break even math
    4. 4

      Is the sample big enough?

      Thirty picks can be luck in either direction. A hot month says little until there are hundreds of graded picks behind it.

      How Watchdog helps

      Every record carries a sample size label: small, developing or established.

      Why sample size matters
    5. 5

      Where are the losses?

      Every real bettor loses often. If you can only find winning tickets, you are not seeing the record.

      How Watchdog helps

      Graded pick ledgers show the losses with the wins.

    Questions

    Before you rely on a record.

    Can I find out who a handicapper is?

    No. Names, account names and account links are not published on the free site or in Watchdog Pro. Each account is a permanent Watchdog ID instead.

    Does Watchdog track every handicapper?

    No. It tracks public accounts it has found on X, Reddit, Covers, TheRX, Sportsbook Review and Predictem. Private groups, paid channels and other sites are not covered.

    Does a good record mean future winners?

    No. A record is history. Results swing, especially on small samples, and a handicapper who is up today can lose tomorrow. Nothing on this site is a promise of profit.

    Can a handicapper pay to rank higher?

    No. Rank comes only from graded results. There are no paid placements, featured rows or verified badges for sale.

    What does a pending pick include in Pro?

    The sport, game, pick and odds as posted, the post time, and the Watchdog ID with its record. Not the name or the original post.

    How often does the site update?

    The board and profiles rebuild from the tracker about every 30 minutes. The time of the last capture is shown at the top of this page.
