## 1. TwoPlusTwo, Sports Betting forum (new thread, data only, no link in the opening post)
Title: Graded 52,000 public picks by the price taken. Heavy favorites won 71% and still lost money.

Body:
I have been grading picks that people post in public (X, Reddit, Covers, TheRX and others), captured before the
game starts and graded on the final score at the price they posted, one unit each. Grouped by the price taken:

- 200 or shorter: won 71.0%, needed 73.9% to break even, ROI minus 3.9%
- 150 to 199: won 59.0%, needed 62.4%, ROI minus 5.3%
- 105 to 120 (standard spread and total prices): won 53.5%, needed 52.7%, ROI plus 1.6%
- Plus 121 to plus 149: won 46.5%, needed 43.1%, ROI plus 7.7%

Across the 104 accounts with 100 or more priced picks: 5 win 52.4% or more and are still down. Of the accounts
that beat the win rate their own prices required, 95% are up; of the ones that did not, none are.

Two caveats. These are people who post in public, not all bettors. And prices of plus 250 or longer are left out:
when I checked them against the market, a lot of them turned out to be player props, futures and parlays that had
been read as team moneylines (Darnell Washington +300 is not the Huskies). Those are held out until each is
checked against its post.

Happy to share the method or run a cut someone is curious about.

(If someone asks where it lives: https://handicapperwatchdog.com/research/odds-and-profit.html)
