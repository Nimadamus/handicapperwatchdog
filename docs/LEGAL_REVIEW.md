# Terms and Privacy: what the owner must review before launch

Both pages (terms.html, privacy.html) are drafts written for this product. They are NOT reviewed or approved.
Nothing on the live site links to them yet; they ship with the Watchdog Pro release.

Decisions that are yours:
1. **Contact address.** Both pages use support@handicapperwatchdog.com, which does not exist yet. Create it (or pick another) before launch.
2. **Refunds.** Draft says no partial period refunds except where required by law. Change if you want a refund window.
3. **Governing law.** Draft says California. Confirm, or name another state.
4. **Liability cap.** Draft caps liability at 12 months of fees. Confirm.
5. **Business name.** Draft says "Handicapper Watchdog". If the Stripe account is under a company name, the terms should name that company.
6. **Grace period wording.** Matches the code: Pro stays on up to 3 days past the period end while Stripe retries a failed payment.
7. **Providers listed in Privacy.** Stripe, Cloudflare, GitHub, Google Analytics, Brevo. If you choose a different email provider, update it.
8. **Age.** Draft requires 21+, matching the site footer.
